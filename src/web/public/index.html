About The RLR Compass

The Real Love Ready Compass is an AI guide to discovering and reflecting on ideas from the RLR library. It offers a new way to explore relationship topics and connect with the people and perspectives behind the content.

How it works

When you ask a question, it searches for relevant passages from the Real Love Ready book, Let's Talk Love, Real Love Ready: The Series, and RLR Substack articles. AI then uses those passages to generate an answer, with references linking you to the sources for deeper exploration.

What it is for

The aim is to support learning and reflection through RLR perspectives, not to draw conclusions about you, your partner, or your relationship.

The Real Love Ready Compass is for education and reflection, not therapy, crisis support, diagnosis, or making relationship decisions.

AI-generated responses may contain mistakes or misinterpret a source. The linked material provides more context and is worth exploring directly.

Still taking shape

This is a private prototype for the Real Love Ready team to explore and review, not a finished product or a fixed direction. Your feedback helps shape what comes next, including what is useful and where answers miss the mark.
